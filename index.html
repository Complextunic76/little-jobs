How it works

Jobs become available when their chosen interval has passed since you last marked them done. An overdue job simply stays available. Nothing sends notifications.

Jobs you've never completed start as available. Use the Job bank to mark recently finished jobs as done or edit any job.

All completion times use your phone's local time. Recurrences are measured from completion, not fixed weekdays.
